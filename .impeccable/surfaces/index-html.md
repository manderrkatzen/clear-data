# ClearData analyst workspace

Mode: Operate. Scope: application shell, dataset overview, single-page review, spreadsheet exploration, decision history, report, and configuration dialogs. The user authorized an overhaul and delegated visual judgment.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A precise analytical review workspace, organized by the analyst's next decision rather than a stack of dashboard cards.

OWN-WORLD: Swiss information-program discipline translated into native product UI: an ink-navy navigation rail, white data surfaces, cobalt selection/actions, explicit amber exceptions and green approvals, Source Sans 3, aligned tabular numerals, restrained six-pixel corners, and meaningful hairline divisions.

STORY: Find suspicious representations, compare missing and present cohorts, and choose a fix while seeing its before/after effect. Approve explicitly on the same page. Unknown, not applicable, and legitimate zero remain distinct.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A compact product rail and dataset bar frame a searchable findings queue and a finding header. Find, Compare, and Fix form a linear question-led page. Value chips, cohort evidence, one selected fix, and one approval action lead. Scope, interpretation, provenance, KPIs, and copilot sit in Advanced; definitions sit on Dataset. Before/after distributions use common bins as the signature comparison. No stepper, separate Preview/Approve screens, or review navigation footer.

FORM: Grounded direction 7, information-program identity systems; seed 9dc53e4a. Code-led, no image generation. Type-specimen discipline raises label alignment; ticket-wallet discipline raises explicit retained/approved/rolled-back states; dark-panel discipline raises density and isolation of destructive actions; acetate-board discipline raises section clarity; atlas discipline raises common chart scales; single-item-feed discipline raises focus on one finding. Dark-panel and ticket-wallet challengers are competitive on clarity but weaker on the daylight analyst scene; other challengers are declined on both identification and clarity as full visual systems. No borrowed decorative motifs.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
